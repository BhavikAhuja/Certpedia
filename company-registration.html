Company types we register

Pick one, we file it

Private Limited Company

Startups and growing businesses that may raise investment.

  • At least 2 directors and 2 shareholders
  • Up to 200 members
  • Liability limited to the shares held
  • Can issue shares to investors

Limited Liability Partnership

Service firms and professionals who want limited liability with lighter paperwork.

  • At least 2 partners
  • Liability limited to the agreed contribution
  • Governed by the LLP Act, 2008
  • No minimum capital
Learn more

Public Limited Company

Larger ventures planning to offer shares to the public.

  • At least 3 directors and 7 members
  • No upper limit on members
  • Can raise money from the public
  • More compliance than other types

Partnership Firm

Small family or trade businesses that want a simple setup.

  • At least 2 partners
  • Run under a partnership deed
  • Partners are personally liable

Company registration questions

Straight answers before you start.

What is company registration?

Company registration, also called incorporation, creates a separate legal entity under the Companies Act, 2013 through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). The company can own assets, sign contracts and sue or be sued in its own name.

Which structure is best for a startup?

Most startups that plan to raise investment choose a Private Limited Company, because it offers limited liability and can issue shares to investors. Founders who want lighter compliance sometimes choose an LLP. The right choice depends on your funding plans and number of founders.

How many directors and shareholders does a Private Limited Company need?

A Private Limited Company needs at least two directors and at least two shareholders, and can have up to 200 members. At least one director must be a resident of India.

Is there a minimum capital requirement?

No. Private and public companies in India are not required to have a minimum paid-up capital. You decide the authorised and paid-up capital that suits your business.

Do I need an office to register a company?

Yes. Every company needs a registered office address in India. You provide proof of the premises, such as a rent agreement or ownership document, a no-objection letter from the owner and a recent utility bill.

How long does registration take?

It depends on name approval, how quickly your documents and digital signatures are ready, and the MCA's processing. After your first consultation we give you an estimate for your specific case.

What should I do after the company is registered?

Open a current account, register for GST if your turnover or business needs it, and apply for MSME registration. Many companies also register a trademark and get ISO or product certifications. We can help with each of these.
