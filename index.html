Questions we hear every day

Short answers about the approvals Indian businesses ask about most.

What is BIS CRS certification?

The Compulsory Registration Scheme of the Bureau of Indian Standards requires manufacturers, including importers, of notified electronic and IT products to register before selling in India. Certpedia coordinates testing, prepares the documents and files the application.

Do I need WPC approval for a Wi-Fi or Bluetooth device?

Wireless equipment sold or imported in India generally needs an Equipment Type Approval (ETA) from WPC, even when it uses licence-exempt bands. Requirements vary by product, so share your model with us and we will confirm what applies.

What is EPR registration and who needs it?

Extended Producer Responsibility registration applies to producers, importers and brand owners under India's plastic waste, e-waste and battery waste rules. We help you register and keep your filings on track.

Which ISO certifications can Certpedia arrange?

We support ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001, ISO 22000, ISO 13485, ISO 45001 and ISO 50001, along with CE marking and ISO surveillance audits.

How long does a certification take?

It depends on the approval, whether product testing is needed and how quickly your documents are ready. After your first consultation we give you a timeline for your specific case.
