# BIS Certification in India: A Guide for Businesses

BIS certification is an important topic for businesses dealing with products in India. Importers, manufacturers and founders should identify the certification requirements that may apply to their products before planning their compliance process.

![BIS certification documents and consumer products in an Indian business setting](/images/blog/2026-10-30-bis-certification.jpg)

## What Is BIS Certification?

BIS certification refers to certification associated with the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). Businesses should identify the applicable product requirements and verify them through official BIS information before proceeding.

## Who Should Check BIS Requirements?

BIS requirements may be relevant to businesses such as:

- **Manufacturers** assessing product compliance requirements.
- **Importers** checking requirements for products they plan to bring into India.
- **Business owners and founders** preparing to launch products.
- **Exporters** reviewing compliance requirements for their business activities.

The exact requirements depend on the product and the applicable official provisions. Do not assume that every product follows the same certification process.

## What Should Businesses Verify?

Before beginning a certification process, review the following:

- The exact product category.
- Whether a BIS requirement applies to the product.
- The applicable Indian Standard, if specified in the official requirements.
- The relevant certification scheme and process.
- The documents and testing requirements specified by BIS.
- Any applicable deadlines or changes stated in the official notification.

## Steps to Prepare for BIS Certification

Follow these steps to assess your next actions:

1. **Identify your product:** Record its name, intended use and relevant specifications.
2. **Check official requirements:** Consult the applicable BIS information and notifications.
3. **Confirm applicability:** Verify whether certification is required for your particular product.
4. **Review documentation:** Identify the documents and testing requirements stated in the applicable scheme.
5. **Check current updates:** Verify any applicable effective dates and changes before proceeding.
6. **Seek professional assistance:** Consult a qualified compliance professional if you need help interpreting the requirements.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 2. Where can I check the applicable requirements?

Check the official BIS information and the relevant notification for your product. Confirm the applicable provisions before taking action.
