Non Network License (WPC)

Get the WPC license you need to own and operate an individual wireless station in India, such as a radio set on a fishing boat. Certpedia prepares the application, files it on the Saral Sanchar portal and follows it through.

What a Non Network License does

Overview

Licenses a single station

It authorises you to own and operate an individual wireless station, rather than a network of many.

Includes marine stations

A maritime mobile station on a fishing trawler or boat is one example filed through the WPC Wing.

Keeps you compliant

Operating radio equipment without the right license can lead to penalties under the wireless laws.

Renewable

These licenses run for a fixed period and need to be renewed. A fishing boat station, for example, is licensed for one year.

Which station types, fees and conditions apply depends on your equipment. We confirm them before we file.

Non Network License questions

Straight answers before you start.

What is a Non Network License?

It is a WPC license, issued by the Wireless Planning and Coordination Wing of the Department of Telecommunications, for owning and operating an individual wireless station rather than a network. A maritime mobile station on a fishing trawler or boat is one example.

What is the difference between a Network and Non Network License?

A Network License assigns frequencies for running your own wireless network over a defined area. A Non Network License covers an individual station that is not part of such a network. We confirm which one applies to your equipment.

Who can apply?

For a maritime mobile station on a fishing boat, any individual fisherman or fishing trawler operator can apply. Other station types have their own eligibility rules, which we check for you.

What documents are required?

For a fishing boat station, usually proof of correspondence address, identity proof of the authorised person, the certificate of registry of the boat, the MMD allotment letter, and details of the previous license with its latest renewal slip. Other station types need different documents.

How is the application made?

Applications are submitted online on the Saral Sanchar portal with the signed application and documents. The WPC Wing scrutinises it, and after you pay the applicable fee you can download the license.

How long is the license valid and what does it cost?

It depends on the type of station. For a maritime mobile station on a fishing trawler or boat, the DoT lists a validity of one year and a fee of Rs 500. Other types differ, and we confirm the current fee and validity for yours.

Do I need other WPC approvals too?

Possibly. Equipment may need an ETA Certificate, importing it may need an Import License WPC, and dealing in it may need a DPL or NDPL. We can tell you which ones apply on a free call.
