Limited Liability Partnership registration

A flexible business structure that combines the limited liability of a company with the simplicity of a partnership. Popular with professionals and service firms. Certpedia handles the filing from name check to your certificate.

Why choose an LLP

Key benefits

Limited liability

Each partner is liable only up to the agreed contribution, so personal assets stay protected.

Separate legal entity

The LLP owns assets, signs contracts and sues in its own name, and continues if partners change.

Flexible management

Partners decide how to run the business through an LLP agreement, with fewer formal meeting rules than a company.

Lower compliance

Compliance is lighter than a company, which keeps running costs down for small and mid-size firms.

LLP questions

Straight answers before you start.

What is a Limited Liability Partnership?

A Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) is a separate legal entity registered under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008. It combines the flexibility of a partnership with the limited liability of a company.

How many partners do I need?

You need at least two partners and at least two designated partners, who must be individuals. At least one designated partner must be a resident of India, meaning someone who has stayed in India for 182 days or more in the previous year. There is no upper limit on partners.

Is there a minimum capital requirement?

No. There is no minimum capital contribution requirement for an LLP in India. Partners agree the contribution that suits the business and record it in the LLP agreement.

What is an LLP agreement?

The LLP agreement sets out the rights and duties of the partners, such as profit sharing, management and exit. It must be filed with the MCA within 30 days of incorporation. We draft it for you.

Can an LLP raise investment from venture funds?

It is harder than for a company. An LLP cannot issue shares, so venture funds and many investors prefer a Private Limited Company. If you plan to raise equity funding, a company is usually the better fit.

What rules apply to the LLP name?

The name must be unique, must not be too similar to an existing company, LLP or registered trademark, and must end with LLP. We check availability before we file.

Can I convert my partnership firm or company into an LLP?

Yes. A registered partnership firm can convert into an LLP, and so can an unlisted company that meets the conditions. We can plan and handle the conversion.
